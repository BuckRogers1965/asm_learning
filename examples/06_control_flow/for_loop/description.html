For loop showing initialization, condition, and increment in assembly.

Key Concepts:

  • Loop Counter: A variable (often in a register) tracks the loop iteration
  • Three-Part Structure: Initialization, condition check, and increment are separate instructions
  • Optimization Potential: Compilers can optimize loops significantly with higher optimization levels
  • Loop Unrolling: At higher optimization, compilers may unroll loops for performance
