Bitwise AND, OR, and XOR operations.

Key Concepts:

  • Bitwise AND: The and instruction performs bit-by-bit logical AND
  • Bitwise OR: The or instruction performs bit-by-bit logical OR
  • Bitwise XOR: The xor instruction performs bit-by-bit exclusive OR
  • Common Uses: Masking bits, setting flags, toggling bits
